- What is NLP?
  it helps computers to understand the human words or language.

- Why does text need to be converted into numbers?
  because machine can understand only the numbers

- What is text preprocessing?
  cleaning the data, structuring,

- What is tokenization?
  breaking the sentence or stream of text into smaller words

- What is vocabulary?
  the collection or mapping of known tokens.

- What are token IDs?
  the numerical identifier assigned to a token.

- What is an unknown token (UNK)?
  when the vocabulary encounters the new words, it might break it process so we initialize the new tokens with unk and assign a token ids for it so it can handle the new words.

- Difference between word tokenization and subword tokenization.
  word tokenization - Splits strictly by spaces and punctuation boundaries. for ex ["smartest"]
  subword tokenization: Splits common words completely, but chops rare/long words into pieces. ["smart", "##est"]
